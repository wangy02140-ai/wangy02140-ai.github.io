01 / ABOUT

At the intersection
of people and design.

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering, specializing in Industrial Design, at Northwestern Polytechnical University, supervised by Prof. Dengkai Chen. I expect to complete my PhD in December 2026.

My doctoral research focuses on cabin lighting design and its effects on operator states and task performance. I combine human factors experiments, multimodal measurements and predictive modeling to support the evaluation and optimization of enclosed operational environments.

I completed one year of joint doctoral research at the National University of Singapore with Ali Ghahramani. Beyond my doctoral research, my project experience spans cockpit environments, crewed spacecraft, manned submersibles and sleep-support products.
